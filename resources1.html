Patient Resources & FAQs

Essential information and guidelines regarding your surgical journey, preparation, and recovery.

Before Your Consult

What records should I bring?

Please bring copies of your past medical records, operative notes, imaging reports, and a list of current medications.

Before Surgery

What medications should I stop taking before surgery?

Stop all blood thinners (Aspirin, Ibuprofen, Coumadin, Eliquis) and herbal supplements (Vitamin E, Fish Oil) 7 days prior to surgery.

When do I need to stop eating and drinking?

Do not eat or drink anything (including water, gum, or mints) after midnight the night before your surgery.

Day of Surgery

What time should I arrive?

Typically, you should arrive 2 hours prior to your scheduled surgery time to complete pre-op preparation.

Do I need someone to drive me home?

Yes. You must have a responsible adult stay at the facility during surgery and drive you home or to your hotel.

After Surgery

When are my follow-up appointments scheduled?

Post-operative visits are scheduled as soon as your surgery is booked. Check your initial scheduling packet for these exact dates.
