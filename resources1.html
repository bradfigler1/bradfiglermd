Patient Resources & FAQs

Structured guidelines and procedural information to help you navigate your surgical journey.

Before your consult Before surgery Day of surgery After surgery

Before your consult

Urethroplasty

Question 1: What records should I bring?

Answer 1: Please bring prior imaging reports, operative notes, and past cystoscopy summaries.

Question 2: Do I need a referral?

Answer 2: Check with your insurance carrier or primary provider regarding formal referral requirements.

Rectourethral fistula repair

Question 1: What diagnostic history is reviewed?

Answer 1: Prior radiation records, lower endoscopy results, and imaging scans are evaluated.

Question 2: How long is the initial consultation?

Answer 2: Consultations typically take 45 to 60 minutes to thoroughly assess complex tissue history.

Buried penis repair

Question 1: What symptoms are assessed during evaluation?

Answer 1: Functional limitations with urination, skin hygiene issues, and anatomical support.

Question 2: Are photos or physical exams required?

Answer 2: A physical examination is typically conducted during your initial visit to map out the surgical approach.

Mohs Reconstruction

Question 1: What pathology reports are needed?

Answer 1: Bring your dermatology or Mohs surgery pathology reports detailing the margins and defect size.

Question 2: When can reconstruction happen after Mohs?

Answer 2: Timing depends on final margin clearance and tissue health following skin cancer removal.

Hidradenitis surgery

Question 1: Do I need a dermatology evaluation first?

Answer 1: Collaborating closely with your dermatologist helps optimize medical management prior to surgical consultation.

Question 2: What areas are typically evaluated?

Answer 2: Active inflammatory lesions and scarring patterns across the genital and perineal regions.

Urethral dilation/Optilume

Question 1: What is the goal of an initial stricture review?

Answer 1: Determining whether minimally invasive options like dilation or Optilume are appropriate versus open surgery.

Question 2: What prior procedural history is helpful?

Answer 2: Dates and outcomes of any previous dilations, direct visual internal urethrotomies (DVIU), or past catheterizations.

Vaginoplasty

Question 1: What mental health documentation is required?

Answer 1: WPATH-aligned letters of support from qualified mental health professionals are typically requested.

Question 2: What surgical techniques are discussed?

Answer 2: We review graft options, anatomical goals, and expected recovery milestones during your consultation.

Metoidioplasty

Question 1: What topics are covered in a planning visit?

Answer 1: Urethral lengthening considerations, scrotoplasty choices, and general functional objectives.

Question 2: Do I need hormone therapy adjustments prior to consult?

Answer 2: Hormone management is reviewed in coordination with your primary provider or endocrinologist.

Phalloplasty (stage 2)

Question 1: What records are required for stage 2 evaluations?

Answer 1: Complete operative notes and timelines from your stage 1 phalloplasty procedure.

Question 2: What goals are addressed in stage 2?

Answer 2: Subsequent reconstructive phases, urinary integration, and secondary aesthetic refinements.

Before surgery

Urethroplasty

Question 1: When should I stop blood thinners?

Answer 1: Discontinue blood thinners and specific supplements 7 days prior to your operation.

Question 2: What if I develop a UTI pre-op?

Answer 2: Notify our office immediately so we can provide targeted antibiotic treatment.

Rectourethral fistula repair

Question 1: Is a bowel preparation required?

Answer 1: Yes, specific mechanical or dietary bowel preps are provided to protect the repair site.

Question 2: When do I start fasting?

Answer 2: No food or drink after midnight on the night prior to surgery.

Buried penis repair

Question 1: Are there special pre-op skin protocols?

Answer 1: Follow any localized antiseptic washing guidelines provided in your pre-op packet.

Question 2: What medications must be paused?

Answer 2: Standard medication pauses apply for anti-platelet agents and herbal supplements.

Mohs Reconstruction

Question 1: How should I care for the Mohs defect pre-op?

Answer 1: Keep temporary dressings clean and dry as instructed by your Mohs surgeon.

Question 2: Can I eat the morning of surgery?

Answer 2: Follow facility fasting guidelines depending on whether local or general anesthesia is used.

Hidradenitis surgery

Question 1: What if I have an active flare before surgery?

Answer 1: Call our clinic right away if you experience sudden acute inflammation or abscess formation.

Question 2: Are there pre-op hygiene regimens?

Answer 2: Specific antibacterial washes are often recommended for the surgical field days prior.

Urethral dilation/Optilume

Question 1: Do I need pre-op antibiotic coverage?

Answer 1: Antibiotics are typically given around the time of the procedure to prevent urinary tract infection.

Question 2: Can I take my regular morning medications?

Answer 2: Blood pressure medications can usually be taken with a small sip of water.

Vaginoplasty

Question 1: What are the hair removal requirements?

Answer 1: Complete all required permanent hair removal protocols well in advance of your surgery date.

Question 2: What bowel prep instructions apply?

Answer 2: Follow your tailored gastrointestinal cleansing regimen the day before surgery.

Metoidioplasty

Question 1: Are hormone adjustments needed?

Answer 1: Review any necessary testosterone timing changes with your surgical care team.

Question 2: When do I stop eating and drinking?

Answer 2: Fasting begins strictly after midnight the night before your procedure.

Phalloplasty (stage 2)

Question 1: What preparation instructions are unique to stage 2?

Answer 1: Specific dressing and hygiene protocols will be outlined during your pre-op visit.

Question 2: What checks are completed before admission?

Answer 2: Final medical clearance and blood work verification must be completed within 30 days.

Day of surgery

Urethroplasty

Question 1: What clothing should I wear?

Answer 1: Wear loose sweatpants or comfortable trousers to accommodate post-op dressings and catheters.

Question 2: Will I wake up with a catheter?

Answer 2: Yes, a urinary catheter is placed while you are asleep to protect the reconstruction.

Rectourethral fistula repair

Question 1: What arrival time is expected?

Answer 1: Typically arrive 2 hours prior to your scheduled operating room time.

Question 2: Do I need a driver?

Answer 2: Yes, discharge requires a responsible adult to accompany you home or to a local recovery hotel.

Buried penis repair

Question 1: Will there be surgical drains?

Answer 1: Temporary drains may be placed to control fluid buildup and are managed during early recovery.

Question 2: Can I bring electronics or personal items?

Answer 2: Bring photo ID and insurance cards; leave valuables and jewelry at home.

Mohs Reconstruction

Question 1: How long does the procedure take?

Answer 1: Duration varies based on the size and complexity of the reconstructive repair.

Question 2: What type of anesthesia is used?

Answer 2: Depending on the site, local anesthesia or sedation is coordinated with your care team.

Hidradenitis surgery

Question 1: What apparel is recommended?

Answer 1: Loose cotton underwear and very baggy pants minimize friction on sensitive regions.

Question 2: What happens during pre-op check-in?

Answer 2: Nurses will start your IV, review your medications, and complete safety verifications.

Urethral dilation/Optilume

Question 1: Is outpatient discharge same-day?

Answer 1: Yes, most dilation or Optilume procedures are performed on a same-day outpatient basis.

Question 2: Will I have a temporary catheter?

Answer 2: A short-term catheter may be placed temporarily depending on the exact procedural approach.

Vaginoplasty

Question 1: What packing or bolsters are placed?

Answer 1: Supportive vaginal packing is placed intraoperatively to maintain tissue pressure and hemostasis.

Question 2: What should my support person know?

Answer 2: Your driver must stay at the facility during surgery and assist with post-discharge logistics.

Metoidioplasty

Question 1: What urinary diversion is utilized?

Answer 1: A suprapubic tube or urethral catheter is typically placed to divert urine safely during healing.

Question 2: What should I wear on arrival?

Answer 2: Soft, loose-fitting loungewear or sweatpants ensuring no pressure on the surgical field.

Phalloplasty (stage 2)

Question 1: What checks occur in pre-op?

Answer 1: Anesthesia review, vital signs confirmation, and site verification with the surgical team.

Question 2: How long is the operating time?

Answer 2: Timelines vary based on the specific secondary reconstructive steps planned.

After surgery

Urethroplasty

Question 1: When is the catheter removed?

Answer 1: Catheters typically remain in place for 14 to 21 days before an imaging check.

Question 2: When can I resume heavy lifting?

Answer 2: Avoid heavy lifting (over 10 lbs) and strenuous exercise for 4 to 6 weeks.

Rectourethral fistula repair

Question 1: How is stool consistency managed?

Answer 1: High-fiber intake and stool softeners prevent straining during bowel movements.

Question 2: When are follow-up visits scheduled?

Answer 2: Initial post-op visits are scheduled at booking to monitor healing milestones.

Buried penis repair

Question 1: How do I manage postoperative swelling?

Answer 1: Supportive underwear and intermittent ice packs help reduce early swelling.

Question 2: When is driving permitted?

Answer 2: Resume driving only when off narcotic pain relievers and comfortable making sudden movements.

Mohs Reconstruction

Question 1: How do I care for incision dressings?

Answer 1: Keep the surgical site clean and dry per your specific post-op wound care sheet.

Question 2: When are sutures removed?

Answer 2: Follow-up suture removal typically occurs within 7 to 14 days post-procedure.

Hidradenitis surgery

Question 1: What signs indicate an infection?

Answer 1: Watch for spreading redness, worsening pain, foul odor, or fever, and call our clinic.

Question 2: How are dressing changes performed?

Answer 2: Follow daily cleansing instructions using mild soap and water or saline as directed.

Urethral dilation/Optilume

Question 1: What urinary symptoms are normal post-procedure?

Answer 1: Mild burning or blood-tinged urine can occur initially and should improve within days.

Question 2: When should I call the office?

Answer 2: Contact us immediately if you experience an inability to urinate or high fever.

Vaginoplasty

Question 1: What does the dilation schedule entail?

Answer 1: Strict adherence to your prescribed dilation routine maintains vaginal depth and caliber.

Question 2: What lubricants are safe to use?

Answer 2: Use water-based lubricants exclusively to avoid irritating healing mucosal tissues.

Metoidioplasty

Question 1: What indicates a good urinary stream?

Answer 1: A steady stream without excessive straining as swelling gradually subsides.

Question 2: When can I return to fitted clothing?

Answer 2: Stick to loose boxer briefs or athletic shorts until acute tissue healing is established.

Phalloplasty (stage 2)

Question 1: What activity restrictions are required?

Answer 1: Strict avoidance of heavy physical exertion and pressure on the surgical zone for several weeks.

Question 2: Who do I contact for urgent post-op concerns?

Answer 2: Reach our on-call provider through the main hospital operator or clinic emergency line.
